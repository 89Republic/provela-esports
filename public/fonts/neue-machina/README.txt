Neue Machina font files

Place your licensed Neue Machina webfont files in this folder.

Recommended (single file):
  PPNeueMachina-PlainVariable.woff2

Or static weights:
  PPNeueMachina-PlainLight.woff2
  PPNeueMachina-PlainRegular.woff2
  PPNeueMachina-PlainMedium.woff2
  PPNeueMachina-PlainBold.woff2
  PPNeueMachina-PlainUltrabold.woff2
  PPNeueMachina-PlainBlack.woff2

Download from Pangram Pangram:
  https://pangrampangram.com/products/neue-machina-plain

If the font is installed on your machine, the app will use it automatically.
